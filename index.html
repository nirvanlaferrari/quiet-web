Room to read.

A full-screen reading view with comfortable typography and a progress line. Full article extraction depends on the source website.

Good reads, saved.

Bookmark stories for later. Previously loaded article text and imported summaries are stored locally for reading offline while the app is open.

Filter by blog, unread stories, or saved favorites. Find your next read without losing your place.
